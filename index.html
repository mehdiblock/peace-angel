Frequently Asked Questions

What does war insurance cover? ▼

Our war insurance covers damages to residential homes caused by bombings, missile attacks, artillery fire, and armed conflicts within the insured territory.

How are claims processed? ▼

Claims are verified through decentralized oracles that pull data from trusted news sources, satellite imagery, and government reports. Once verified, payout is automatic.

Why do I need to connect my wallet? ▼

Your wallet serves as your identity and payment method. All transactions and policies are tied to your wallet address on the blockchain.

Is my personal data safe? ▼

Absolutely. We collect no personal data. No KYC, no email, no phone number. Your wallet is your only identifier.
